About us

Social media, run like a sales channel.

Fable & Co. plans, films, produces and manages short-form content for brands that expect their marketing to pay for itself. We measure it against reach, sales and footfall. Not likes.

We work hands-on with a small roster of brands across very different niches, from a late-night taverna in Goa to a mythology storytelling page with 243K followers. Videography, editing and design all happen under one roof.

Growth on social is rarely a viral accident. It's a routine, repeated until it compounds. So every engagement starts with a paid audit, and if we don't believe we can move your numbers, we'll tell you then and part as friends.
